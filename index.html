Swarmheart Privacy Policy

Kurogaya. Effective October 7, 2026.

Swarmheart is a game for Android and iPhone, made by the Bonewick Games studio and published by Kurogaya ("we"). This policy explains what information the game and its ads use.

What the game itself collects

Nothing. Swarmheart has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. Your progress, settings and saved runs stay on your phone. They are deleted if you uninstall the game.

Ads

The phone version is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, for example to get back up once in a run, to double a run's gold, or for gold in the shop;
  • now and then, a short ad between runs.

Swarmheart asks Google for non-personalised ads only. These ads are chosen from things like the game itself and your general location, not from a profile of you.

To show, count and protect these ads, Google's software in the game may still collect:

  • your IP address, which can show your approximate location;
  • your device's advertising identifier and app set ID (on Android);
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic information, such as how the ad software performs.

This data is sent securely (encrypted). Google uses it to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, count views and prevent fraud. How Google handles it is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent message before any ad loads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings > Ad Choices.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings under Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).
  • On iPhone, Swarmheart does not ask to track you.
  • The game works fully offline, and no ads load without a connection.

Children

Swarmheart is made for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and want something removed, contact us.

Deleting your data

We keep nothing about you on any server of ours. Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on your phone. For data held by Google, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
